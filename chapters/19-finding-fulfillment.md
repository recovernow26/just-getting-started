# Day 19 — Finding Fulfillment

*November 20, 2026*

For most of my life, I chased better things — jobs, money, clothes, cars, positions — only to be left broken inside. Nothing I had was ever good enough. I would complain and often get pissed off if I couldn't get what I wanted. After I married Amy, I thought I'd hit the jackpot. I was the happiest man alive, and we didn't have much of anything besides each other. Little did I know then that contentment would fade because of unresolved issues with my mind. It wasn't that I didn't love her or the kids. I truly loved that woman with my entire heart. It was because I chose behaviors that stole the integrity between us.

So I started chasing the better life again. I became lost inside my own mind, and it tore us apart. She was betrayed in a lot of ways. She trusted that she fulfilled me, and most of her trust was true. It was the lies and choosing of other things that damaged my dignity. Her loyalty alone was precious enough to fulfill me.

I missed a lot of moments with her and the kids that I can't get back, and that is painful to know. Brushing her hair, dancing, being still in the moment, taking the kids to the doctor — all of which is enough to make me feel and be whole. Holding her hand brought me more joy than any car ever could. Seeing her smile genuinely is more satisfying than any house would allow.

So now, even after the loss of time and years of damage caused, I'm learning to be focused on the moments that matter. To be fully conscious is to be fulfilled.

Amy, sweetheart, I'm sorry for breaking your heart. I pray I have the opportunity to be present in your life in a good way. For now, I'm learning with vigilance to heal my distorted mind, so you won't suffer another injury from me. I love you to the moon and back.
