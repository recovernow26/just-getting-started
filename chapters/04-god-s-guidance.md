# Day 4 — God's Guidance

*November 5, 2026*

After all the times I've turned my back on God, He's never ignored my return. I've actually been guilty of using the Bible as a weapon instead of following the principles offered within. Refusing to acknowledge His pleading, direction, and wisdom cost me dearly. Refusing to humble myself blurred my vision further. As a result, the paths I traveled down were dark and lonely. I used God's abundant grace and mercy like a get-out-of-jail-free card. Thank goodness He never abandoned me. After I hit rock bottom, He was right there to pick me up.

"Can you see me now?" He asked. I replied, "Yessir. I'm sorry for being stupid." He said, "Son, you're not stupid. You lost your way. Come on, let's go this way."

I'm learning to trust God. He's teaching me how to trust myself. How to build my faith. How to preserve my confidence. Life is tough and sometimes I get lost. I'm glad He is always there to show me the way.

"If My people, who are called by My name, humble themselves and pray and seek my face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear them from Heaven, and forgive their sin and heal their land."

2 Chronicles 7:14
