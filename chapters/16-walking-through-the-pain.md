# Day 16 — Walking Through the Pain

*November 17, 2026*

I can't tell you how many times I promised myself and others I would never do drugs again. I was sincere in my heart at the time, but I was sick — plagued with a disease that consumes your mind, sick with twisted perceptions that distorted the fabric of reality. Addiction was to blame.

I was exposed to pornography at age nine or ten, and I was persuaded to become sexually active around the same time. I had feelings I'd never felt before, and to be honest, I was hooked. It wasn't long until I was introduced to alcohol and drugs. By twelve years old, I was lost in the world of addiction. My behavior was very volatile, and I spiraled deeper into being an introvert.

I was already pretty much alone from birth — at least, that's how I remember it as of now. By five years old, I would often stay in the woods all day by myself. I thought that was what life was. I guess that is why I was so impressionable.

It was a terrible journey going through all of the situations I found myself in, yet nothing was worse than the pain I caused myself by not realizing reality. Truth is, I was to blame for my perception. I even chose to stay in those realms instead of opting out. One time was on them; the repetition of going back was on me. I see that now more than ever.

The pain that was inflicted on others because of my choices caused a lot of regret, and out of regret came more pain. I kept myself locked in prison, isolating my mind from the chance of healing. People did try to help, but they weren't in my mind, and I was silent on the actual condition of my mental state.

After years of burned bridges, I'm waking up slowly — by design. I'm becoming conscious. Pain still exists, and I'm not emotionally mature to the degree I desire. One day, I will look back and be proud of the perseverance God gave me to climb back out of the hole I was in. Then I can honestly say that I walked through the pain.

Thank you, God, for giving me grace. I praise you for the courage I have. I pray for full restoration and complete healing.
