# Day 15 — Alone No More

*November 16, 2026*

I've isolated myself for most of my life, whether by substance abuse or fear of connection. I desired to be around people, yet I chose not to become someone worth being around. I would convince myself that I wasn't good enough, that no one cared anyway, becoming very self-centered. I then began to believe that everyone else was the problem, or that I hadn't found the right people. I never did. The spiral continued until I was almost dead. My energy was next to zero, my life in shambles. I was hopeless, sick, and alone.

It took a lot of time to start seeing the truth about how and why I was so lonely, how I got there, what I had done, and the people that I hurt. That I was actually the person that abandoned society through escapism and fantasy. My heart was broken. Empathy hit me like a ton of bricks, crushing what I had left of my soul. My mind tormented by the thoughts of the damage I'd done. Regret crept in and overwhelmed me to the point of physical pain. If I had woken up sooner, I told myself, as I begged God for another chance. Losing grip of the people that brought me joy and purpose scared me to death. They were never mine to hold.

Somehow, I believed that I was in charge of the lives of my wife and children, that I could do what I wanted and have finite expectations holding them intrusively accountable, while being a man with no morals or principles personally. The gap between us grew wider each day. It took them leaving for change to begin taking root. You don't know what you got till it's gone.

I'm in recovery now at RSR, located in Rockmart, Georgia. Amy left back in July 2026. These past few months have been tough for me, but not as tough as it has been for my family. I have to become solid in order to be effectively beneficial to their lives. I understand that now more than ever.

My mind is getting clearer and more efficient. I'm establishing goals and creating a daily itinerary that I intend to honor holistically. The NA group meetings are a huge help. Together with other training and sincere devotion to my recovery, they are reframing my character. As a result, my relationship with Amy and the children is starting to blossom. I'm also beginning to establish new connections with people. I can honestly say that I'm falling in love with life because I'm no longer alone. Just for today, I will stay focused on the process that is restoring my life.
