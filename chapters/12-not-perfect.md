# Day 12 — Not Perfect

*November 13, 2026*

This is a great topic to reflect on. I admit that I have demanded perfection from people around me while I was a mess myself. Pointing out others' flaws like I was leading a team of scientists — arrogant, condescending and self-righteous. It was easy for me. I mean, why look in the mirror and point out my flaws when there were obviously more people with more issues. Surely I wasn't the problem. As you can tell, I had plenty of friends and people that loved to be around me.

It wasn't until my wife had had enough and decided to leave that I started to reconsider my way of thinking. I mean, she tried so hard and was undeniably patient with me. Problem was, I used her for her grace and stole her dignity by acting like a saint out in public while treating her and the children like garbage. This realization was mortifying. They say hindsight is 20/20, but I felt like a double zero. Then came the victim mentality at first, for a while at least, until I really sat with my thoughts in the ashes of my doing. Alone.

I'm beginning to get better. Empathy, well defined, is the engine driving my perspective. Selfless sadness is correcting my emotional immaturity. Hopeful reconciliation is taming my reckless spirit. Divine intervention is restoring my mind and body. True love is making me whole. Just for today I will hold my peace, so that others can receive compassion just as I have experienced. Thank you, God, for not destroying the very flawed human that I am.
