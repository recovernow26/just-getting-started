# Day 10 — From Surrender to Acceptance

*November 11, 2026*

It took a while for me to surrender. Honestly, I can't say for sure that I'm fully so. At least not completely. Not in every area of my life. I must admit, I'm quite stubborn. I've definitely made progress towards that goal. It's tough to navigate the corridors of the mind and extract the roots that anchor systemic issues to our spirit. Some of them may be considered life rafts in my consciousness.

The NA meetings and group sessions at RSR have offered a means to explore the caveats that lurk behind years of towering walls built by emotional behaviors and toxic paradigms. I have begun to learn new skills that are tearing down those walls, paving the way to the full scope of my mental disease. As the process leads to more progress, the gap between surrender to acceptance is closing. Bridging my mind back to reality and offering hope for complete freedom, liberty and wholeness. For this reason I am grateful. God is restoring my mind, my body and my spirit day by day.

God grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, the courage to change the things I can, and the wisdom to know the difference.
