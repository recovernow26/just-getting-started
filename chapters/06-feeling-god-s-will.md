# Day 6 — Feeling God's Will

*November 7, 2026*

In the past, I've been guilty of searching for signs that I was "on the right track." I would go into a store and purchase a few items, and if the total cost landed on certain numbers I would take that as confirmation that my timing was spot on. If I had a hard time connecting with people throughout the day, I would ask God what I was doing wrong and believe that I made a wrong turn. This led to self condemnation and desperation. Ultimately, this way of thinking put my mind in a box that limited my potential. My direction stole my confidence, and damaged relationships.

I know now that it is not for my knowledge to "know" what tomorrow holds. The more I "know," the more opportunity I have to mess my life up. I understand that as I heal and become more grounded, I am changing into a person that attracts blessings. I believe that is God's will for me. Why would He want me to fail? Why would He want me to stay confused?

So, just for today, I choose to continue the work that is providing change. Just for today I choose to remain clean. Just for today I choose to trust God with the will for my life, as I learn to trust myself.
