# Day 14 — Letting Go

*November 15, 2026*

I've held onto past events, resentment, failures, and pain so long that I became cynical. I convinced myself that my experience was everyone else's fault. I was the victim of a sick and twisted world with no escape. Then came the reckoning.

I had a tough childhood full of physical and emotional abuse. An unstable family fraught with drugs and alcohol created a toxic perception of reality. Ultimately, it was my decisions that led to the chaotic storm in my life. Choosing to hold on to the narrative others were living cost me precious time and completely drained my energy. Making poor choices because of being lazy stole my success. Blaming others for my undesirable situations caused me to remain chained to grief. Not letting go of the past kept me grounded in despair.

I had to lose people that I sincerely cared about to fully accept I was wrong. The ones in my life that truly loved me had to sit quietly until I woke up from the coma I put myself in. I thank God for real, genuine love. I'm most grateful for faith and hope.

As I let go of the pain and resentment I carry, I'm becoming alive. My mind is getting stronger with focus at the center. My body is becoming connected with my brain. My spirit is starting to thrive on progress. Life has begun to be naturally enjoyable. Letting go of the past is compassion. Enlightenment is to be fully conscious and aware of the present. Total engagement is the catalyst that fuels the future.

Take my will and my life. Guide me in my recovery. Show me how to live clean.
