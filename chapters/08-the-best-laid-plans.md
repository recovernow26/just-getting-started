# Day 8 — The Best Laid Plans

*November 9, 2026*

Not getting what I want when I want it often made me angry. I even began to believe that if I had plans or had expectations in my mind, they were going to happen. Somehow, someway, if I did what I had to do throughout the day, I deserved to get what I wanted. Eventually I wasn't doing what I had to do. Not providing, not treating people with respect, not taking care of myself, but I still made plans in my head and with others and was certain those plans would come to pass. I was in a fantasy world. I can't imagine what others thought of me.

It took a while to get out of that mindset. To understand my mindset. To fully wake up to this realization. It's okay to have desire. It's okay to have an idea about what I want my life to look like. It's not okay to assume I'm in control of the world I live in. I'm truly working on myself so that my mind becomes clearer. More present, grounded in God's principles. In that, I will become a man that strives to be balanced, prepared, and patient. I'm on God's time and He has my future under control. The journey I am on is subject to the reality I choose. Good or bad.

Whatever the circumstances are, I choose to trust God, and to walk out my due diligence with respect, with honor, with dignity and with integrity.
