# Day 1 — Living with Unresolved Problems

*November 2, 2026*

As I reflected on this, I chose to focus on what led me here in the first place. Looking back, the main issue wasn't just that life was hard. It was that I kept choosing behaviors that occupied valuable time, behaviors that led to zero productivity, and behaviors that brought me nowhere. Day by day those unresolved problems accumulated like interest on a loan I couldn't afford to pay. Layer by layer they accumulated until I found myself existing in a state of despair.

But reflecting on the current state of affairs, I don't see it as just a negative. Looking forward, I hope to view every new problem as a challenge and a chance to gain new knowledge. I plan to reframe my mental state through the most available and appropriate channels, to handle each challenge with confidence, and to hopefully never again accumulate problems and clutter in my mental space. I will be more careful to engage in behaviors that are more productive.
