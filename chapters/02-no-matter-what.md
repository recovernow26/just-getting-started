# Day 2 — No Matter What

*November 3, 2026*

Not being real with others can be a reflection of not being honest with yourself. Let's face it, life can be brutal and undesirable to keep in the forefront of our minds, much less to continue conversing about the topics that tend to weigh us down. In fact, if we were more able to share our struggles, past or present, we could gain access to others' experiences and potentially receive guidance. Not talking about real life is only a form of escapism, and I believe this robs not only oneself of growth but could also remove hope from the atmosphere that is designed to offer true friendship and camaraderie.

Furthermore, without dwelling on our worst-case life story, we can share the truth and move forward so our minds, time and energy can give place for our wins, victories, and accomplishments. In that space, joy, peace and love can thrive.
