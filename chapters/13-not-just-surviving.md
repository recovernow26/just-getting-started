# Day 13 — Not Just Surviving

*November 14, 2026*

I used to believe that surviving was not a choice if you wanted to keep going. If I stopped, I was going to die. That I had no options. I had life figured out, and it was "it is what it is." There I was again, confident in my own ignorance. Over and over, I repeated cycles that left me broken and confused.

I would get a fresh start, a great job. I climbed up rung by rung, getting a vehicle, a house, and started providing for my family. At least in my mind. On the other hand, I would consume alcohol, take drugs, and occasionally steal things when I got the urge. I know — what a great guy, right? I couldn't figure out why I felt like crap all the time. I was justified in my actions; surely nothing was wrong with my character. My back would hurt, so I ate pain pills. I wanted to relax, so I drank beer and liquor. I needed a tool or food, so I stole them. I was doing what I had to do to survive. But what world was I in? Did I really enjoy the environment I created?

As I began to ask myself these questions, a light came on — but it was too late. The fruit of my labor caught up to me. I was addicted to opiates. I was behind on my rent and owed money out to so many places I couldn't count. My body was weak and feeble because of abuse and neglect. My relationships were toxic and volatile, to say the least. Further down I went, until I was homeless with nothing, or in jail because of my actions. The cycle continued: I would get out of jail with the best intentions, only to begin living the same way as before.

This last experience was real bad. I had a woman that loved me completely, kids that adored me, and the best circle of people I'd ever had. I betrayed them all, and you guessed it — back to zero: in jail, wife gone, house auctioned, and nothing to my name but dishonor.

I've begun to climb again, slowly. I want it this way. No more fast tracking success as I have known in the past. I can say that I've begun to notice parts of my life that are so wonderful. I'm captivated by the little things in a way that satisfies my soul. I'm content with the progress I am making. I'm also striving to continue advancing. I'm clean — that makes a difference. Just for today, I will stay clean. I will choose to walk with integrity. I will choose to honor my higher power. I will choose to live instead of just surviving.
