# Day 20 — Letting Our Defects Go

*November 21, 2026*

It's easy to see others' defects, so much so I became self-righteous. Quick to point out others' flaws, I became reclusive, not wanting to be around anyone. Come to find out, the hiding was because of my personal issues. Heck, when I started to see myself in the mirror, I was mortified. Instead of getting help, I self-diagnosed and ended up condemned. Reaching for any fix that would offer relief from reality drove me to near psychological collapse. I ran after options that hurt a lot of people, and most of all myself. I even at one point believed there was no hope. Despair is a dark place filled with monsters and snakes. Walking through that hell almost killed me.

If it wasn't for my adorable wife Amy, I wouldn't be here writing this ink on the page today. Her long suffering is the most courageous act I've ever seen in my life. Her wisdom, earned through years of torment and abuse, was the catalyst that ultimately brought me to my senses. Unfortunately, she had to separate from me for the seeds she had planted to begin to take root, and by then she was near complete exhaustion, depleted of her goodness because she spent it all on me. Thankfully, God kept her safe, and she is getting better every day. Every day is a fresh start for both of us, and our communication is getting more intimate.

We didn't choose to be together for our shortcomings. We fell apart because of my choices. Letting go of my failure and choosing to be a better man is redefining our relationship. My hope is strong. My faith is growing from true integrity. I pray that strength will continue to be found, with confidence that honor will be fully restored, dignity completely felt, that love will abound.
