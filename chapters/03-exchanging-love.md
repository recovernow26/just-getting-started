# Day 3 — Exchanging Love

*November 4, 2026*

How can I love someone honestly if I don't fully love myself? I have failed at this, mostly because I didn't know what loving myself really was. I used to give myself anything and everything I desired in the moment. I would often get myself into situations that I hated. Then I would convince myself that someone else was to blame. I had no healthy boundaries, no standards. My morals were terrible. My health was unimportant. I chose drugs and alcohol over my family. As a result I stayed desperate and hopeless. My wife tried to wake me up but I was stubborn. Looking back I can see that her effort robbed her of her love.

Love should be reciprocated. Love doesn't betray, lie, boast, envy or steal. Love doesn't ignore. Love isn't selfish or self-centered. Love is thankfulness and gratitude converted into actions. Kindness, patience, self control, education, sacrifice, and discipline should be present in my life always.

After healthy habits and behaviors are formed within my life, I can then begin to form healthy relationships. I can repair the damage I caused between myself and others.

In that atmosphere, true peace and joy will be abundant. I can experience the life I've always wanted. To be happy. To truly be loved. Because we keep what we have by giving it away!
