# Day 11 — Our Own Story

*November 12, 2026*

I've often thought of my story selfishly. I've been very egotistical and conceited. Lacking compassion for the people I hurt. Never once did I have the capacity for empathy. It was my way or the highway. I want what I want and I want it now, no matter what the consequence.

Looking back I can see I was exactly right. I wanted chaos. I chased a feel-good, on my terms lifestyle. Following the expert advice of my ego, I became the king of my world. Broke, busted, disgusted and alone. Still had my ego, as he was my best friend. After a period of emotional fallout, my body and spirit formed an alliance and began searching for something better. Desperate for human connection and stability, at last something shifted.

I was learning to sit in discomfort. To look at the wreckage for what it was. To be sincerely present in reality. Looking at myself through other people's perspective, I was mortified. I said to myself, this story you've been living has been undesirable because of you. No one else was to blame for my failure.

Shifting mindsets has been challenging but very enlightening. Repairing the damage has become honorable. As new opportunities arise, I can finally say I have forgiven myself. The future is near, the past is at the rear. Nevertheless, the present deserves no fear.

I'm 38 years old, and this story — my story — is just getting started.
