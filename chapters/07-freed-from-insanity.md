# Day 7 — Freed From Insanity

*November 8, 2026*

I have chosen to be insane most of my life. Insanity is continuing to do the same things expecting a different outcome. Deep down I wanted a different experience in life. I wanted peace that was sustained. Joy that came naturally. Genuine, healthy relationships. Good health. A natural positive attitude. However, I chose to remain stuck. I remained in the emotional storm. I chose substances to elevate my moods. Behaviors that kept my mind cloudy. I would make excuses and tell myself that I would change tomorrow.

Tomorrow came and I would continue to choose the drugs, alcohol, pornography and even spending money instead of choosing my wife, my children, my job, and valuing my personal well being. I had to hit rock bottom to wake up. I mean, I lost my business, my possessions, my house, my beautiful wife and children. I lost my good reputation, my friendships, my dignity, and almost lost my life.

By the grace of God I have been given an opportunity to reframe my life, and I am waking up to a better mentality. I hope I never lose myself to the point of insanity again.
