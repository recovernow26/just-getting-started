# Day 9 — Fear or Faith

*November 10, 2026*

Fear has crippled my mind at times in my life. I would often abandon my faith in myself. I believe now that I was consumed by fear as a consequence of the choices I made. Betraying my sweet wife, being lazy, choosing to consume drugs and alcohol, watching porn. These habits stole my confidence and destroyed relationships on a level that hasn't been fully understood by me. My behavior really damaged my mind, body and spirit. My choices led to undermining my faith and others' faith as well.

Correcting these issues has been imperative to me at this stage of my life. Ensuring I remain grounded in Godly principles is a must. Becoming a different man — of value, honesty, love, hope, introspection, dignity, honor, confidence, and faith — has become my due diligence. I am very excited and encouraged about the reframing of my life. Living with optimism has become a lifestyle. I know that the damage I caused will take time to repair. I abused the love and faith that others invested in me, and I see that clearly now.

What was broken by me will reconnect. With repetition I will remain solid. With faith I can learn to face fear head on, with the skills and mindset to achieve success.

"Fear is a compass. The cave you fear to enter holds the treasure that you seek."
