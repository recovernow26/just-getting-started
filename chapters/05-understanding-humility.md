# Day 5 — Understanding Humility

*November 6, 2026*

To be honest, I've struggled with being real with myself throughout my life. I often suppressed the mistakes I made and at times completely disregarded the facts that were before me. Running from the consequences of my actions only created more issues. I became very depressed and hopeless as a result. It took a while to wake up to reality. I give my wife credit for remaining present with me in my ignorance. It's tough to look back and see that the behavior I exhibited took away precious moments of our life.

My current focus is directed towards examining my past and correcting thought patterns that were unhealthy. Every day brings new challenges for sure. With proper observance and correction, I'm confident I can become a new man. Healthy habits will ensure sustained progress. Remaining honest will guarantee accepting life on life's terms.

Stay honest, stay teachable, stay positive, and stay motivated. Stay determined to be humble.
